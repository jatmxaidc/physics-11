# Class 11 Physics: CBSE vs NEET

## Basis of comparison

- **CBSE:** Physics (Code 042), Class XI syllabus for the 2026–27 session.
- **NEET:** Official NMC/UGMEB NEET-UG 2026 syllabus dated 22 December 2025.
- “Extra” below means explicitly named in one official syllabus but not explicitly named in the other. A broadly worded topic may still cover some named examples.

## Overall conclusion

All 14 CBSE Class 11 Physics chapters are represented in the Class 11 portion of NEET Physics. The conceptual overlap is high. Students should complete the CBSE/NCERT syllabus first and then add NEET-specific points and objective-question practice.

## Practical and experimental comparison

### NEET Experimental Skills
- NEET has a separate Experimental Skills unit containing selected Class XI and XII experiments.
- Questions are objective and may test apparatus, principles, observations and interpretation.
- Relevant Class XI examples include Vernier calipers, screw gauge, pendulum, elasticity, surface tension, viscosity, resonance tube and calorimetry.

### CBSE Class XI Practicals
- Practical examination: 30 marks
- Two experiments: 7 + 7 marks
- Practical record: 5 marks
- One activity: 3 marks
- Investigatory project: 3 marks
- Viva: 5 marks
- Students maintain records of at least eight experiments and six activities.

## Recommended study order

1. Complete all NCERT theory, examples, exercises and CBSE derivations.
2. Add the explicitly listed NEET points, especially:
   - Least count and errors
   - Relative velocity
   - Conservation of mechanical energy
   - Parallel- and perpendicular-axis theorems
   - Satellite time period
   - Experimental skills
3. Practise chapter-wise NEET MCQs and timed mixed tests.
