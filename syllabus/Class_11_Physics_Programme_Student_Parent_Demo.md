# A Clear Goal for Class 11

Class 11 Physics is the foundation for Class 12, NEET and other science entrance examinations. Many students struggle because the syllabus is completed late, difficult chapters are rushed, and there is insufficient time for revision.

This programme follows a different structure:

- Complete the full Class 11 syllabus by December
- Build concepts from the basics
- Prepare simultaneously for CBSE and NEET
- Test learning after every lecture
- Review test mistakes before starting the next topic
- Identify and correct weaknesses early
- Reserve January for revision and mock examinations

The programme is suitable for students who want strong school results while developing a serious foundation for NEET.

# What Students Will Receive



## Strong Conceptual Teaching

Every chapter begins with fundamental ideas and progresses towards applications and numerical problems. Mathematical tools required for Physics are taught before they are used.

## Complete CBSE Preparation

- NCERT theory, examples and exercises
- Important definitions and graphs
- Step-by-step derivations
- Written numerical solutions
- Correct answer presentation
- Practical and viva concepts
- CBSE-style chapter and full-syllabus tests



## NEET Foundation

- Chapter-wise NEET MCQs
- Timed numerical practice
- Formula application
- Accuracy and speed development
- Experimental Skills questions
- Cumulative and full-syllabus mock tests



## Personal Error Tracking

Each student maintains an error log containing:

- Conceptual mistakes
- Formula mistakes
- Calculation mistakes
- Reading and interpretation mistakes
- Time-management problems

Incorrect questions are corrected and attempted again so that mistakes become learning opportunities.

## Daily Practice Test

After every lecture, the student receives a short online test containing approximately **5–10 questions** from the topic taught that day.

The daily test checks:

- Understanding of the new concept
- Correct use of formulas
- Numerical calculation
- Application to CBSE and NEET questions
- Recall of important definitions, graphs or results

The student completes the test after class. Before a new topic begins the following day, the teacher reviews the test data and discusses the most important incorrect or difficult questions.

This creates a continuous cycle:

**Learn → Attempt → Analyse → Correct → Continue**

## Teacher-Side Topic Analytics

Every daily-test attempt is recorded. The teacher can analyse performance by student, chapter, topic and question.

The analysis includes:

- Test completion status
- Score and percentage
- Accuracy by topic
- Correct and incorrect questions
- Frequently repeated mistakes
- Time taken, when available
- Strong and weak concepts
- Improvement across multiple tests

This allows the next lecture or remediation session to respond to actual student performance rather than assumptions.

# How Each Lecture Works



## Concept Lecture 

- **10 minutes:** Review priority questions from the previous daily test
- **30 minutes:** New concept and derivation
- **15 minutes:** Guided numerical problems
- **5 minutes:** Summary and daily-test instructions



## Practice Lecture

- Formula and concept recall
- CBSE written questions
- Timed NEET MCQs
- Solution discussion
- Error analysis

After class, the student completes a **5–10 question daily test**. Its results determine the questions reviewed at the beginning of the next lecture.

This structure ensures that students do not simply watch lectures—they learn, attempt, receive feedback, correct errors and then progress.

# Assessment and Progress System

## Daily Evaluation

After each lecture:

1. A 5–10 question test is assigned.
2. The student completes it before the next lecture.
3. The platform records the attempt and question-level responses.
4. The teacher reviews topic-wise performance.
5. Priority mistakes are solved before the next new topic.
6. Students needing additional support receive targeted practice or remediation.



## Weekly Evaluation

Each week includes:

- Six short daily tests
- A timed NEET MCQ set
- A CBSE written answer or derivation
- Homework review
- Error-log review
- A topic-wise teacher analysis



## Cumulative Evaluation

Every three to four weeks, students attempt a cumulative test containing both recent and older chapters. This reduces forgetting and improves long-term retention.

## Final Evaluation

The programme includes:

- One full-syllabus diagnostic test
- Two sectional examinations
- One Thermal Physics cumulative test
- One NEET-style Class 11 Physics mock
- One CBSE full-syllabus mock
- Two weak-chapter remediation clinics

- Chapter score below 70%
- Repeated low daily-test scores

- Repeated difficulty in the same topic
- Increasing number of unresolved errors
- Weak calculation speed or written presentation

Support is based on actual student performance rather than waiting until the final examination.

# Responsibilities of the Student

To benefit fully, the student should:

- Attend classes regularly
- Complete the 5–10 question daily test before the next class
- Read the assigned NCERT pages
- Complete homework on time
- Maintain a formula and derivation notebook
- Maintain an honest error log
- Correct test mistakes within 48 hours
- Ask questions whenever a concept is unclear
- Revise the weekly formula sheet every Sunday

Recommended self-study after each lecture is approximately 30–45 minutes.

- Completed important NCERT examples
- Attempted the assigned exercises
- Completed the daily topic tests
- Prepared formulas and derivations
- Completed a chapter test
- Corrected all identified mistakes
- Scored at least 70% or attended remediation

# Final Message to Students and Parents

Success in Physics does not come from memorising formulas or watching a large number of lectures. It comes from understanding concepts, practising consistently, correcting mistakes and revising at the right time.
