# Class 11 Physics Lecture Plan: CBSE + NEET Foundation

## Plan settings

- **Start date:** 24 August 2026
- **Completion deadline:** 15 January 2027
- **Frequency:** 6 lectures per week
- **Lecture duration:** 60 minutes
- **Target:** CBSE Class 11 annual examination plus NEET foundation
- **Total planned sessions:** 125
- **Core syllabus:** 97 sessions
- **Chapter/unit tests, revision and buffer:** 28 sessions

The core syllabus is completed by **21 December 2026**. The remaining period is used for tests, backlog clearance and the first full revision before 15 January 2027.

## Standard structure of each 60-minute lecture

- 5 minutes: recall quiz and review of homework
- 35 minutes: concepts, derivations and demonstrations
- 15 minutes: guided CBSE numerical plus NEET-style MCQs
- 5 minutes: summary, formula recall and homework assignment

## Weekly study outside lectures

- 30–45 minutes of Physics self-study after each lecture
- NCERT reading before the next lecture
- 15–25 questions after each completed topic
- One error-log update after every test
- One formula-sheet revision every Sunday

# Testing system

## Chapter tests

- 20–30 minutes
- 40% CBSE written questions and derivations
- 60% NEET-style MCQs and numericals

## Unit tests

- 60 minutes
- Include previously completed chapters to prevent forgetting
- Maintain separate scores for accuracy, speed and presentation

## Full mock tests

- One diagnostic test before revision
- One NEET-style Class 11 Physics mock
- Two CBSE-style cumulative/mock examinations

## Progress rules

- Target at least **70%** before moving from a chapter test.
- Reattempt every incorrect numerical within 48 hours.
- A chapter is complete only after NCERT examples, NCERT exercises, formula revision and a chapter test.
- Use Week 18 buffer sessions for school holidays, illness or unfinished difficult topics.
