## How a day is planned

| Kind of day | When we use it | Clock |
|---|---|---|
| **Full 60 min** | New idea, student trap, or already-dense PW lecture | 8 min DT review · 32 min concept · 15 min numerical · 5 min close |
| **Shared 60 min** | PW split one idea across two days, or a short add-on | 8 min DT · 22–30 min title (1) · 15–25 min title (2) · 10 min mixed numerical |
| **Practice** | End of each chapter | Full hour of CBSE + NEET items + 70% gate |
| **Lock** | 3–10 Feb, Mon–Fri | Mixed papers, mock, formula lock |

If a shared day overruns, **finish title (1) properly** and move title (2)’s numericals to the next-day DT.
